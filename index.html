Instructor-facing overview

Adaptive judgment is easier to teach when students can see it happen.

The Adaptive Species Labs are a sequence of small decision worlds. Students change the conditions around a problem, watch the system respond, and then explain what the pattern reveals about adaptation under uncertainty.

The labs are designed for quick demonstrations, full class activities, or reflective assignments. Each lab includes controls, a visualization, dashboard feedback, quick/deep modes, and reflection prompts that move students from observation to transfer.

Teaching rhythm

Run the default state first. Change one condition. Trigger stress. Read the dashboard. Then ask students what the pattern teaches beyond the simulation.

1. RunBegin with the default world.
2. ChangeMove one variable at a time.
3. StressTrigger a shock or stress test.
4. InterpretUse metrics as clues, not grades.
5. TransferConnect the pattern to real decisions.
