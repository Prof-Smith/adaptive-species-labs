Welcome to the Adaptive Species Labs

These labs are designed as small decision worlds. Each one lets students change the conditions around a problem, watch the system respond, and then reflect on what the simulation reveals about adaptive judgment.

Rather than treating uncertainty, bias, cooperation, AI, and ignorance as abstract concepts, the labs make them visible. Students can test strategies, compare outcomes, and practice the habits that help people and institutions learn before consequences become irreversible.

Experiment

Adjust sliders, trigger shocks, run stress tests, and see how small changes reshape the decision environment.

Interpret

Use the dashboards, maps, and visual feedback to separate process quality from outcome luck, signal from noise, and adaptation from overreaction.

Reflect

Each lab includes prompts that connect the simulation back to classrooms, organizations, markets, AI workflows, and personal decision systems.

Teaching rhythm: run the simulation first, ask students what changed, then connect the pattern back to the chapter’s central principle.
