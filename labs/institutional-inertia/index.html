The question

Why do institutions stay on a failing path?

Institutions can know change is needed and still fail to move. Habits, investments, routines, politics, and coordination costs can keep the system moving in the old direction even after the environment has shifted.

Lab rule:
Inertia is not simply stubbornness. It is the weight of past commitments acting on present choices.
