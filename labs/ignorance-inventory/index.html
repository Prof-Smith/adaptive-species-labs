The question

Can you manage uncertainty before it manages you?

Chapter 13 turns ignorance into something visible. The goal is not to eliminate uncertainty. The goal is to classify it well enough to act with humility, curiosity, and guardrails.

Lab rule:
Unknowns become less dangerous when you name them, rank them, and decide what would reveal them.

Inventory model

How to read the four zones

Known facts

Evidence you can currently defend.

Assumptions

Beliefs holding the decision together.

Known unknowns

Questions you know how to ask but have not answered.

Unknown unknowns

Surprises that may not be visible until the environment changes.
