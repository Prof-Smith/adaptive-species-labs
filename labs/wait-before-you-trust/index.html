The question

When does AI extend judgment, and when does it replace practice?

Chapter 12 treats AI as a new environment for judgment. The key question is not simply whether AI improves the product. The deeper question is whether AI improves the person who uses it.

Lab rule:
Use AI to extend judgment, not to evacuate it.
