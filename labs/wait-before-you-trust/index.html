Chapter 12 · AI as a New Environment for Judgment

WAIT Before You Trust

A visual AI-reliance simulator showing how delegation to AI can improve speed while weakening inspection, transfer, and human skill if the workflow removes judgment.

Lab rule: Use AI to extend judgment, not to evacuate it.

The Question

When does AI support adaptive judgment, and when does it replace the very practice that builds judgment? This lab simulates a student or professional using AI across repeated work cycles. The goal is not to avoid AI. The goal is to design the workflow so human skill, inspection, and transfer remain alive.

Chapter 12 connection

Chapter 12 can treat AI as a new decision environment. The question is not only what AI can produce. The question is what AI use trains, weakens, hides, or amplifies in the human decision maker.

Guide to the WAIT Framework

The lab uses four checkpoints. Higher WAIT discipline means the user keeps agency inside the AI workflow. Lower WAIT discipline means AI output becomes fast, smooth, and potentially unexamined.

WorkflowWhere does AI enter the process, and what remains human-owned?
AssumptionsWhat premises, missing context, or hidden defaults might AI be using?
InspectionHow carefully does the user verify claims, logic, and fit?
TransferDoes the user learn something they can carry into the next task?
Teaching move: Ask students whether AI improved the product, the person, both, or only the appearance of competence.

Reflection Prompts

  • Where did AI enter the workflow?
  • Which assumptions needed human checking?
  • What did inspection catch or miss?
  • What skill transferred to the next task?
  • Did AI make the student faster, wiser, or merely more polished?

Chapter Connection

This lab follows optionality because AI also changes the option set. It can open new possibilities, but it can also close the option of learning if it removes too much struggle, inspection, and transfer.

The adaptive question is not whether to use AI. The adaptive question is how to keep judgment in the loop.
