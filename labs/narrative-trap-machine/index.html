The question

When does a useful story become a trap?

Stories help people organize complexity. But when the pull toward coherence becomes too strong, confirming clues rush inward, ambiguous clues are absorbed, contradictions are pushed away, and missing evidence becomes easy to ignore.

Lab rule:
A good story is not the same thing as good evidence.
Step 1
