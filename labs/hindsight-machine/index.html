The question

How do outcomes rewrite memory, confidence, blame, and learning?

After an outcome occurs, the past can look more obvious than it was. This lab shows how a result can cast a shadow backward over the original decision.

Lab rule:
Do not let the ending steal the uncertainty that existed at the beginning.
Step 1

Set the review environment

Use three controls. They determine the quality of the pre-outcome process, the force of the result, and whether review preserves learning.
