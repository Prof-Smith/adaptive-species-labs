The question

When does review preserve learning, and when does review rewrite the story?

After an outcome occurs, the past can look more obvious than it was. This machine shows how a review system can sort cases by process and outcome, or distort them by overreacting to the ending.

Lab rule:
Do not let the ending steal the uncertainty that existed at the beginning.
