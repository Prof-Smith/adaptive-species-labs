The question

When does a good adaptation become a trap?

A strategy can improve locally and still miss a better peak. It can fit yesterday’s terrain and fail after the environment shifts. This lab makes adaptation visible as movement across a changing landscape.

Lab rule:
Adaptation is not climbing forever. It is knowing when the landscape has changed.
Step 1

Set the landscape

Use three controls. Ruggedness changes the terrain. Exploration changes willingness to leave the current hill. Environmental change moves the terrain underneath the agent.
