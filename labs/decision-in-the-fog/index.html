The question

How do you act before the ending is known?

Chapter 1 begins with the human condition: decisions arrive before certainty. This lab turns that condition into a small decision world. The goal is not to guess the future correctly. The goal is to preserve the quality of your judgment before the future answers.

Lab rule:
Do not judge the decision from the ending. Reconstruct the mind that had to act before the ending was known.

Step 2

See the fog map

The map is not a prediction engine. It is a visual reminder that decisions contain facts, assumptions, unknowns, risks, opportunities, and update triggers at the same time.

Path A · Commit

Move now. Useful when evidence is strong or the window is closing.

Path B · Stage

Take a reversible step. Useful when evidence is mixed and stakes matter.

Path C · Wait

Delay for more evidence. Useful when information is poor and time allows.
