The question

How do you act before the ending is known?

Chapter 1 begins with the human condition: decisions arrive before certainty. The goal is not to guess the future correctly. The goal is to preserve the quality of judgment before the future answers.

Lab rule:
Do not judge the decision from the ending. Reconstruct the mind that had to act before the ending was known.
Step 1

See the fog map

The moving lines show possible paths. The drifting fog and arrows show environmental forces. As the sliders change, the weather around the decision changes too.

Environment: mixed evidence · narrowing window · meaningful stakes

Path A · Commit

Move now. Best when evidence is strong or waiting is becoming costly.

Path B · Stage

Take a reversible step. Best when evidence is mixed and stakes matter.

Path C · Wait

Delay for more evidence. Best when information is poor and time still allows.
