The question

When does cooperation become adaptive?

Cooperation is not softness. Under the right conditions, cooperation is a strategy for repeated uncertainty. This lab shows how reciprocity, temptation, and noise change whether cooperation can survive.

Lab rule:
Cooperation survives when future interaction matters more than short-term exploitation.
Step 1

Set the cooperation environment

Use three controls. Future shadow rewards repeated trust. Temptation rewards short-term defection. Noise creates accidental betrayals and misread signals.
