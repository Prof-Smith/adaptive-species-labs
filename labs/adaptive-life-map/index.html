The question

Is your system able to keep learning?

Chapter 14 is the synthesis. The adaptive life is not a perfect plan. It is a loop that keeps contact with reality, chooses under uncertainty, acts before certainty, learns from feedback, and renews capacity.

Lab rule:
The adaptive life is not a fixed path. It is a system that can sense, revise, and renew under changing conditions.
