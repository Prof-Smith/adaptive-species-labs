The question

When does a forecast stop being useful?

A model can fit the past and still miss the present. This lab separates three things that students often collapse: the baseline forecast, the uncertainty band, and the moment when the world changes enough that the old pattern no longer deserves trust.

Lab rule:
A forecast is a disciplined bet, not a promise. Update when the regime changes.
