The question

What deserves attention before it becomes urgent?

Attention is an adaptive resource. It helps people respond to threats and opportunities, but it can be pulled toward the loudest signal rather than the most important one. This lab makes the tradeoff visible.

Lab rule:
What gets attention gets adaptive capacity. What gets ignored becomes surprise.
Step 1

Set the attention environment

Use three controls. Attention budget limits how much can be monitored. Salience pull makes loud signals more attractive. Slow-signal importance controls how much value is hidden in quieter signals.
